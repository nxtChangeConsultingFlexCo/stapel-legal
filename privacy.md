# Privacy Policy for Stapel

Last updated: 12 October 2026

## In short

Stapel has no account, no server and no analytics. Your receipts, the recognised line items and the
categories stay on your iPhone. We receive no data from you. The only outside connection the app makes
is to the App Store, for buying Stapel Plus. If you set it up, the app also files PDFs into a folder
you choose in the Files app, for example in iCloud Drive or OneDrive. That folder is yours, not ours.

## What the app stores

The app stores on your iPhone:

- your receipts: the original as a PDF (from photos the app builds a searchable PDF), a small
  preview image, the recognised text, issuer, date, total, tax rates, the individual line items with
  quantity and price, type and category, note, warranty and payment deadline, and the file name
  under which a receipt was filed,
- what you teach the app about categories: which category you last gave an issuer or a product,
- how many receipts you have added in the current month (for the free limit),
- a bookmark to the filing folder you chose,
- whether Stapel Plus is active.

This data lives only in the app’s protected storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone
else. If you delete a receipt, it is gone; if you delete the app, all receipts are gone. PDFs you
filed into your cloud folder stay there.

Receipts can contain details about other people, such as the name of a tradesperson or a doctor,
and bills from doctors or pharmacies say something about your health. You decide which receipts you
photograph. The app processes them only on your device and passes nothing on.

If iCloud Backup is turned on on your iPhone, Apple backs up the app’s data together with the rest of
your device. Apple’s privacy policy applies to that.

## Camera, Photos and Files

Stapel uses the camera only when you photograph a receipt, through the document scanner of iOS. The
app takes no pictures in the background.

From your photo library the app receives only the pictures you tap in the iOS picker. It does not see
the rest of the library. Location data that photos may contain is not carried into the PDF.

Files from the Files app, from Mail or from the share sheet of other apps are read only when you hand
them to the app. What you send to Stapel through the share sheet is placed by the share extension into
an inbox on your device; the app reads it the next time it opens and empties it.

## Text recognition and on-device language model

The app reads the text of a receipt with the text recognition of iOS (Vision), on your iPhone. On
iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later), Apple’s on-device language model additionally helps
to structure line items and total. Both happen without an internet connection. No image and no text
leaves your device for this, not even to Apple.

## Filing into your cloud folder (Stapel Plus)

With Stapel Plus you can choose a folder into which the app files receipts as PDFs. You choose it in
the Files app of iOS; the folder can be in iCloud Drive, in OneDrive, in Google Drive or with another
provider whose app you have installed. Stapel only writes into this folder and reads nothing from it.
The file name follows a template you set in the app, for example from the receipt’s date, issuer, subject and
total, such as “2026-10-12 Billa 47.80.pdf”.

What happens to the file in the folder is governed by the respective provider under its contract with
you and its privacy policy: Apple for iCloud Drive (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), Microsoft
for OneDrive (https://privacy.microsoft.com), Google for Google Drive
(https://policies.google.com/privacy). We have no influence on it and no access. You can remove the
folder in the app at any time; the app then files nothing more.

## Notifications

If you enter a warranty or a payment deadline for a receipt, the app offers to remind you in advance:
30 days before the warranty ends, 3 days before payment is due. iOS schedules these reminders on your
device; there is no server sending them. The first time, iOS asks whether the app may show
notifications. You can turn them off at any time under iPhone Settings › Notifications › Stapel.

## Purchases (Stapel Plus)

You buy Stapel Plus through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We receive no payment details, no
address and no name. The app only asks the App Store whether a valid subscription belongs to your Apple
Account and remembers the answer on your device. Apple’s privacy policy applies to Apple’s processing:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

The legal basis is the performance of the contract with you (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).

## Crash reports

If you have allowed sharing analytics with app developers in the iOS Settings, Apple may pass
anonymised crash reports of the app on to us. They contain technical details about the crash and the
device, but nothing from your receipts. You control this under iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security ›
Analytics & Improvements.

## What the app does not do

- no user account, no registration,
- no transfer to our own servers, there are none,
- no analytics, advertising or tracking services,
- no sharing of data with third parties,
- no access to location, microphone or contacts,
- camera and photos only when you add a receipt,
- no reading of your cloud folder, only writing, and only if you chose one.

## Contact and your rights

Since we do not process any personal data of yours, there is nothing with us that we could disclose,
correct or delete. You delete your data in the app yourself by removing receipts or deleting the app;
PDFs in your cloud folder you delete there.

Should we ever process personal data, for example because you e-mail us, you have the rights under the
GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. We keep e-mails you
send us for as long as answering them requires. Complaints can be lodged with the Austrian Data
Protection Authority: https://www.dsb.gv.at
