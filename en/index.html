iPhone app

The pile sorts itself.

Photograph a receipt or an invoice, done. Stapel reads line items, total and date on your iPhone, sorts the receipt into a category, shows you month and year and files the original as a PDF in your cloud folder. No account, no server.

Coming to the App Store

How it works

  • Photograph a receipt, pick it from Photos or share a PDF with Stapel.
  • The app reads issuer, date, total, tax rates and every line item, on the iPhone.
  • It checks whether the items add up to the total and shows you where something is off.
  • A category per receipt and per item; the app learns what you correct.
  • Overview by month and year, issuer and product. Price history: “butter in July +18 %”.
  • Enter a warranty and a payment deadline; Stapel reminds you in time.
  • With Plus: the original as a searchable PDF in iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Google Drive, named by date, issuer and total.

No account. No data. No ads.

No account, no server, no analytics. Text recognition and the language model run on the device. Nobody sees your receipts but you, and the cloud folder is yours.

Honestly

Recognition can be wrong. That is why Stapel always shows whether the items add up to the total, and every value can be changed. Stapel is an overview for your home, not bookkeeping.
