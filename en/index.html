The pile sorts itself.

Photograph a receipt or an invoice, done. Stapel reads line items, total and date on your iPhone, sorts the receipt into a category and files the original as a PDF in your cloud folder. No account, no server.

How it works

  1. 01Photograph a receipt, pick it from Photos or share a PDF with Stapel.
  2. 02The app reads issuer, date, total, tax rates and every line item, on the iPhone.
  3. 03It checks whether the items add up to the total and shows you where something is off.
  4. 04A category per receipt and per item; the app learns what you correct. Every receipt sits on a stack, such as Personal or Business.
  5. 05Overview by month and year, issuer and product. Price history: “butter in July +18 %”. Stapel reminds you of warranties and payment deadlines in time.
  6. 06With Plus: the original as a searchable PDF in iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Google Drive, named by date, issuer and total. And a ZIP per period for your accountant.

Personal and business, kept apart

Every receipt sits on a stack, such as Personal or Business. At the top of the list you switch between your stacks or see them all together. The overview and the CSV export follow the stack you chose, and if you like, its name also goes into the file name of the PDFs in your cloud folder.

One stack is free, more come with Plus. Even with a stack for your business, Stapel stays an overview, not bookkeeping; that is what your accountant does.

Hand-over to your accountant Plus

Stapel packs the receipts of a stack for a month, a quarter or a year into a ZIP. Inside is one folder per document type: purchase invoices, sales invoices, cash receipts, bank statements and other, plus a CSV with date, issuer, amount and tax rates. You upload the ZIP to your accountant’s client portal, such as RZL or BMD, or to any other accounting software. Handed-over receipts are marked in the list, so next time you only send the new ones.

Stapel has no connection to your accountant. You pass the ZIP on through the iOS share sheet, wherever you want. Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you send it.

No account. No data. No ads.

No account, no server, no analytics. Text recognition and the language model run on the device. Nobody sees your receipts but you, and the cloud folder is yours.

Honestly

Recognition can be wrong. That is why Stapel always shows whether the items add up to the total, and every value can be changed. Stapel is an overview for your home, not bookkeeping.
